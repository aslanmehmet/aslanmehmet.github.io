Privacy Policy: Seed of Sabr Publishing Tool

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Seed of Sabr Publishing Tool ("the tool") is a private application used only by the owner of the Seed of Sabr channel to publish their own videos to their own YouTube channel and Instagram account.

What data the tool accesses

  • The basic profile information of the owner's own Instagram professional account and YouTube channel (account name and ID).
  • The ability to publish videos and captions created by the owner to the owner's own accounts.
  • Statistics (views, likes, comments) of the owner's own posts, to plan future content.

What the tool does not do

  • It does not access, collect or store data about any other person or account.
  • It does not read or send private messages.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not use data for advertising.

Google user data

The tool's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How data is stored

Access tokens and post information are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by the tool.

Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the tool at any time by removing it in Instagram (Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites) or in their Google account settings, and by deleting the local token files. No other copy of the data exists.
